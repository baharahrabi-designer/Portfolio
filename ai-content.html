The Process

01

Start with the product

Every project begins from actual product photography. AI extends what exists, never invents from nothing.

02

Prompt with brand language

Prompts are written using the brand's visual vocabulary so every output feels native to the brand.

03

Iterate until it fits

AI generation is an iterative craft. Each output is refined until it matches the editorial quality expected from traditional photography.

04

Art-direct and deliver

Final images are selected, cropped, and sequenced into cohesive content sets ready to deploy.
